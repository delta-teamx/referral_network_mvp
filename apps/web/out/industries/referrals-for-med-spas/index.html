A referral network built for med spas

Med spas grow on trust and word of mouth. Referral Nova uses AI to match you with the dermatologists, salons, and wellness partners who send high-value clients - and tracks every referral, so growth is not left to ad spend.

Who this is for

Med spa owners and aesthetic practices that want a dependable stream of high-value client referrals from complementary providers and local businesses.

The problem

Many med spas lean heavily on paid ads with rising costs and thin loyalty. The relationships that send the best clients - dermatology practices, salons, wellness studios - are underdeveloped and untracked.

How Referral Nova helps

  • AI matches you with dermatologists, salons, fitness studios, and wellness providers.
  • Warm introductions build trusted, two-way referral relationships.
  • Every referral is tracked from partner to booked, paying client.
  • Reduce dependence on paid ads with a compounding referral engine.

What results to expect

A steadier flow of pre-qualified, high-value clients; stronger partnerships with local providers; and growth that is not hostage to ad costs.

Frequently asked questions

How do med spas get more client referrals?
Med spas grow fastest through referrals from complementary providers - dermatologists, plastic surgeons, salons, gyms, and wellness practitioners - plus happy clients. Referral Nova matches you with those partners using AI and tracks the referrals both ways, so growth is not dependent on ad spend alone.
What is the best referral partner for a med spa?
The best referral partners for a med spa are businesses that already serve your ideal client and do not compete with your core services: dermatology and plastic surgery practices, hair salons, fitness studios, and wellness providers. Referral Nova finds and matches those partners for you.
Can med spas track where referrals come from?
Yes. Referral Nova tracks each referral from the partner who sent it through to a booked, paying client, so you know which relationships actually drive revenue.
